# Search intent and content briefs

- **Intent types [Practice]:** informational (learn), navigational (find a site), commercial investigation (compare), transactional (do/buy), local. A page should serve one dominant intent; mixed intent usually needs separate pages.
- **Establish intent from evidence:** what currently satisfies the query (look at result types: guides, product pages, tools, videos), query wording, Search Console queries for existing pages, support tickets and site search logs. Do not assume.
- **Keyword-to-page map:** one primary topic/intent per indexable page, supporting terms and questions as sections. Record: URL, primary query, intent, target audience, current status, owner. Two pages for one intent compete (cannibalization): merge, differentiate or redirect.
- **Content brief template:** working title, primary intent and audience, key questions to answer (from data and subject experts), required sections and order, evidence/sources to cite, internal links to include (to and from), calls to action, media needed, structured-data candidates, what NOT to cover (scope), word count only as a rough scope guide, review/approval owner.
- **Quality bar:** original information or clear synthesis, accurate and sourced claims, up-to-date, author/reviewer attribution where it matters (health, finance, legal topics need qualified review), no thin pages created only for keywords.
- **AI-assisted drafting:** allowed to help, but accuracy, originality and usefulness are the publisher's responsibility; mass-producing unreviewed pages risks violating spam policies [verify current guidance].
- **Freshness:** update when facts change; change `dateModified` and visible dates only for real revisions.
- **Local and product intent:** see `seo-international-ecommerce` for locale and catalog pages.
