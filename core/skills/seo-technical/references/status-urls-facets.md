# Status codes, redirects, URLs and facets

- **Status semantics:** 200 indexable content; 301/308 permanent redirect; 302/307 temporary; 404 not found; 410 gone (deliberately removed); 5xx server errors (sustained 5xx can reduce crawling; 503 with `Retry-After` for planned maintenance). Soft 404 = a 200 page that says "not found" or is empty: fix to a real 404/410 or redirect when a close replacement exists [Guideline].
- **Redirects:** one hop to the final URL, no chains or loops, permanent redirects for permanent moves, keep them in place long term (commonly at least a year, [Practice]), redirect to the most relevant equivalent page rather than the homepage; update internal links to the final URL. Server-level redirects (web server, CDN) beat JS/meta refresh [Guideline].
- **URL design [Practice]:** readable, lowercase, hyphen-separated, stable, no session IDs, minimal parameters, consistent trailing slash, one canonical host/protocol; avoid changing URLs without a reason and a redirect map.
- **Parameters:** tracking and sort parameters should not create indexable duplicates: canonicalize to the clean URL, strip tracking at the edge when possible, keep internal links parameter-free. Google's URL Parameters tool was retired, so rely on canonicals, robots rules and link hygiene (verify current state).
- **Faceted navigation (e-commerce/listings):** decide which facet combinations have search demand and unique value (index them with clean URLs, unique titles, self-canonical, in sitemap); the rest are crawl-controlled: not linked as crawlable links where possible, `noindex` (if crawling allowed) or robots disallow for infinite combination spaces (not both on the same URL, because blocked URLs cannot show `noindex`). Limit filter depth, sort orders and pagination combinations.
- **Pagination:** each page self-canonical and crawlable with unique URLs; Google does not use `rel=prev/next` as an indexing signal (verify), but links between pages remain essential; avoid canonicalizing all pages to page 1 unless a "view all" is the real equivalent.
- **Case, encoding and trailing slashes:** redirect variants to the canonical form; test non-ASCII URLs encoding.
- **Internationalization:** locale URL strategy and hreflang are covered in the international/e-commerce SEO skill (P4); do not auto-redirect by IP or Accept-Language in a way that hides alternates from crawlers.
- **Headers:** `Link: rel=canonical` for non-HTML; `X-Robots-Tag` for files; `Vary` handled correctly for dynamic serving; consistent `Content-Type`.
- **Verification:** crawl with a crawler tool or script, list status codes and redirect hops, compare with sitemap and canonical targets.
